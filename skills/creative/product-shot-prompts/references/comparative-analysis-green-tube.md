# Comparative Analysis: My Prompt vs Better Model Prompt

## Context
User showed a prompt from another model that outperformed mine for the same green tube product (SYNDA^GE Age-Defense SPF 50).

## MY PROMPT (rejected):
> "A photorealistic luxury skincare campaign image of the product floating at a subtle tilt in a soft pale sage-green environment, a thin beam of warm white light cuts diagonally across the product from upper-right to lower-left creating a sharp luminous line on the tube surface, a translucent ribbon of soft warm light curls gently around the product in a loose spiral, its edges glowing faintly where the light catches it..."

## BETTER MODEL PROMPT (user approved):
> "Professional product photography of a dark forest green cosmetic tube floating and suspended in mid-air, centered against a soft, light pastel green gradient background. A narrow, focused beam of light strikes the tube from above at a slight angle, creating a crisp highlight along its surface and a soft shadow beneath it. Thin, warm-white translucent light wisps and glowing particles swirl gently around the product, curling like smoke or light trails, adding a sense of elegance and motion."

## Key differences that made it better:

1. **Movement nouns vs static objects:** "wisps" + "particles" (dynamic) vs "ribbon" (dead thing)
2. **Similes:** "like smoke or light trails" gives the model a visual anchor
3. **Movement verbs:** "swirl gently" + "curling" vs simpler "curls around"
4. **Feeling:** "adding a sense of elegance and motion" — emotional direction
5. **Spatial clarity:** "suspended in mid-air" + "soft shadow beneath it" — clear relationships

## Core Lesson
Image models respond to **MOVEMENT WORDS + SIMILES + FEELING**, not to static object descriptions. "Ribbon" is a thing. "Wisps curling like smoke" is an action the model can visualize and render.
